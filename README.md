# openmobisim

**An open-source simulator for planning and optimising multimodal mobility hubs and the
transport networks they connect, built for strategic planning and design optimisation.**

> **Status: pre-alpha, under active development.** Nothing here is ready for use yet, and
> everything may change without notice. External contributions are not accepted at this stage.

## What it will offer

- **Multimodal mobility hubs at the centre**: the places where people switch between public
  transport, private and shared vehicles, and active modes.
- **Strategic planning**: comparing network and hub designs (where to place hubs, how large they
  should be, which services they offer) and their effect on how people travel.
- **Optimisation-ready**: fast and reproducible enough to be evaluated many times inside
  optimisation and scenario-analysis workflows.
- **Easy to use**: a Python interface on a fast core, installable with `pip`.
